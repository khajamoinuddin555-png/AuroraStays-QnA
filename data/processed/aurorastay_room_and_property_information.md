## 1. Room types and occupancy

Room names describe the bed configuration and major features shown on the property page. They do not guarantee a specific floor, view, connecting room, or accessible feature unless the reservation confirmation or a documented request states it. The property may assign an equivalent room when the reserved room type is unavailable, subject to manager review.

| Room type       | Typical configuration                          | Typical occupancy                                                | Important note                                                                            |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| King            | One king bed                                   | Up to 2 adults, or 2 adults and 1 child where local limits allow | Rollaway availability and fees vary by property.                                          |
| Double Queen    | Two queen beds                                 | Up to 4 guests, subject to fire code and property limits         | Do not promise a crib, rollaway, or connecting room without confirmation.                 |
| Accessible King | King bed with selected accessible features     | Up to 2 adults, subject to property limits                       | Ask which features are needed. An accessible room is not a medical accommodation package. |
| Suite           | Bedroom plus living area; configuration varies | Varies by property and suite type                                | Confirm bed count, sofa bed, kitchen, and maximum occupancy in the live profile.          |

Occupancy limits follow the property profile, local fire and building requirements, and the room's posted capacity. Staff must not suggest that a guest exceed the stated capacity. When a guest's group size changes, recheck the room and rate before confirming.

## 4. Parking, pets, and service animals

Parking is not guaranteed unless the reservation or property profile says it is reserved. Staff must quote the current parking type and charge, explain whether in-and-out access is allowed, and tell the guest how to validate or pay. Parking charges are separate from the room rate unless the confirmation says they are included.

Pet rules are property-specific. A pet fee, weight limit, number limit, room restriction, and vaccination or control requirement may apply. Service animals are not pets under the ADA and are not subject to a pet fee. Staff may ask only the two ADA-permitted questions when the disability-related need is not obvious: whether the animal is required because of a disability and what work or task it has been trained to perform.

An emotional support, comfort, or therapy animal that does not meet the ADA service animal definition is handled under the property's pet rule unless another law or approved accommodation applies. Do not request a certificate, vest, or medical record as a condition of recognizing a service animal.

## 6. Accessibility and guest requests

AuroraStay provides reasonable modifications and accessible features as required by applicable law. A guest may request an accessible route, mobility feature, visual or audible alert, communication support, or another accommodation. Staff should ask what feature is needed, record the request in the approved system, and confirm what the property can provide.

Do not treat a request for an accessible room as an optional preference when the guest is asking for an accessibility feature. Do not disclose the guest's disability details to staff who do not need them. If the request cannot be met as stated, involve the manager on duty and offer a specific alternative without making a promise the property cannot deliver.

Service animals may accompany a guest in areas open to guests, subject to the ADA rules for control, housebreaking, and direct threat. A property may remove an animal that is out of control or not housebroken when the issue is not corrected, but staff must not charge a pet fee or make a decision based only on breed or appearance.

## Escalate immediately

Escalate a request when a guest reports a blocked accessible route, an inaccessible room feature, a service animal dispute, a safety risk, or a potential discrimination complaint. Record the request and the response in neutral factual language.
