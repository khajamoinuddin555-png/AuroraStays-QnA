## 1. Purpose and refund principles

This guide helps guest service staff explain refunds, releases, credits, and additional charges. The reservation rate terms control eligibility. The payment method and booking channel control how the money moves. Staff must not promise a refund before checking both.

Refunds generally return to the original payment method. A different method may be used only when Finance or a manager approves it and the transaction can be documented. Cash refunds are not the default for card or agency payments.

## Three questions before quoting a refund

What was charged? What rule makes it refundable or non-refundable? Who can release or approve the funds? If any answer is unclear, say that the request is under review and give the guest a follow-up channel.

## 2. Common refund scenarios

| Scenario                                      | Baseline outcome                                                                                                                  | Required verification                                                                  |
|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Flexible reservation canceled on time         | No room charge. If a prepayment was taken, refund the eligible amount after verifying the cancellation timestamp.                 | Rate plan, local property deadline, booking channel, and payment status.               |
| Flexible reservation canceled late or no-show | One night plus applicable tax may be retained under the baseline rule.                                                            | Exact cancellation time, guest notice, property time zone, and any approved exception. |
| Advance Purchase cancellation                 | Normally non-refundable. Escalate only with a documented contract, system error, legal requirement, or approved service recovery. | Confirmation terms, payment capture, manager approval, and reason for exception.       |
| Duplicate or incorrect charge                 | Correct the duplicate or error and refund the excess after reconciling the folio.                                                 | Folio, payment ledger, authorization, and whether the booking channel also charged.    |
| Service recovery                              | Manager may approve a partial refund, credit, or other remedy based on the documented impact.                                     | Complaint facts, remedy offered, approver, amount, and guest acceptance.               |

## 3. Additional charges

Charges must be disclosed in the rate, confirmation, property profile, posted notice, or guest agreement before they are applied, except for documented damage or loss assessed under the guest's agreement and applicable law. Staff must provide an itemized folio or receipt on request.

| Charge                        | Typical baseline                                              | When it may apply                                                                     | Guest explanation                                                                                                                 |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Parking                       | Property-specific nightly amount                              | Vehicle uses on-site valet, self parking, or an approved partner garage.              | Quote type, nightly amount, tax, and whether in-and-out access is included.                                                       |
| Pet fee                       | Property-specific per stay or per night                       | A non-service animal stays under the property's pet policy.                           | State amount, limit, room restriction, and whether tax applies. Do not charge service animals a pet fee.                          |
| Late checkout                 | Property-specific; may be a flat fee or additional night      | Guest receives an approved departure extension.                                       | Confirm the new departure time and exact fee before approval.                                                                     |
| Smoking or vaping remediation | Up to $250 baseline, subject to evidence and local law        | Credible evidence shows smoking or vaping in a prohibited guest room or indoor area.  | Explain the rule, evidence category, charge basis, and dispute channel. Do not charge based on odor alone when facts are unclear. |
| Lost key or device            | Reasonable replacement cost when stated in the property terms | A guest does not return a physical key, parking device, or other property asset.      | State the item, replacement basis, and how the guest can return it or dispute the charge.                                         |
| Damage or missing item        | Documented reasonable repair or replacement cost              | Inspection supports damage beyond ordinary wear or an item is missing after checkout. | Provide an itemized explanation and dispute path. Do not disclose another guest's information.                                    |

The $250 smoking or vaping amount is an internal baseline only. A property may publish a different amount if the guest agreement and applicable law support it. Do not invent a fee that is not in an approved source.

## 4. Incidentals authorization and release

At arrival, the property may authorize an amount for anticipated incidentals. The authorization can reduce available credit without being a posted charge. When the folio is settled, the property sends a release for the unused amount. The card issuer controls how quickly the pending hold disappears; AuroraStay cannot guarantee a same-day release.

If a guest sees a pending hold after checkout, staff should confirm the final folio, explain that the unused portion was released or is scheduled for release, and advise the guest to contact the card issuer if the pending status persists beyond the issuer's normal timeframe. A pending authorization is not evidence of a second final charge.

## 6. Refund workflow and dispute handling

|   Step | Action                                                                                               | Evidence to retain                                                       |
|--------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|      1 | Locate booking channel, rate plan, folio, and payment records.                                       | Confirmation, folio, payment status, cancellation or checkout timestamp. |
|      2 | Identify the policy basis for a refund, retained amount, or charge.                                  | Rate terms, property profile, signed agreement, or manager decision.     |
|      3 | Process the approved transaction in the payment system or route to the channel that collected funds. | Transaction reference and amount. Never store full card data.            |
|      4 | Tell the guest what was done and when the funds should appear.                                       | Guest communication, promised follow-up date, and escalation owner.      |
|      5 | Escalate an unresolved dispute to Finance, the manager on duty, or the booking channel.              | Issue summary and prior contacts, without sensitive data.                |

A cardholder who reports an unauthorized charge should be directed to the official contact channel and the card issuer. Staff should not ask the guest to send card numbers, CVV codes, passwords, or identity documents through ordinary email or chat.
