## 1. Scope and governing rules

This policy applies to individual transient reservations made through an AuroraStay website, call center, property, authorized travel advisor, or approved online travel agency. The reservation confirmation controls when it contains a rate rule that is more specific than this baseline. Group, negotiated corporate, government, wholesale, crew, and extended-stay agreements may have separate written terms.

The reservation record must show the property, arrival and departure dates, room type, number of guests, rate plan, payment method, cancellation deadline, taxes and disclosed fees. Staff must read the exact rate plan before promising a cancellation, change, refund, upgrade, or fee waiver.

## Guest-facing summary

Flexible rates can normally be canceled by 6:00 PM local property time one day before arrival without a room charge. Advance Purchase rates are generally non-refundable and cannot be changed. The confirmation controls if it states a different rule.

## 3. Changes, no-shows, and special circumstances

A guest may request a date, room type, occupancy, or property change. Staff must price the modified reservation using the current available rate and verify whether the original rate plan permits the change. A modification is not complete until the new confirmation is issued.

If a guest does not arrive by the property cutoff, the reservation is treated as a no-show unless the guest has arranged a late arrival. The baseline charge is one night plus applicable tax for flexible rates. A multi-night reservation may be released after the first night unless the property has received payment or written notice for the rest of the stay.

If a property cannot honor a confirmed reservation because of an overbooking or an operational closure, the manager on duty must arrange a comparable room when feasible, cover approved reasonable relocation costs, and document the resolution. The manager must not describe a relocation as a guest cancellation.

A manager may approve a one-time exception for a documented service failure, duplicate booking, verified system error, or other approved service recovery. The reason, approver, amount, and guest communication must be recorded in the reservation record.

## 4. Taxes, deposits, and guarantees

| Item                      | Baseline rule                                                                                                                          | What to tell the guest                                                                                         |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Room and occupancy tax    | Applied according to the property location and current tax setup.                                                                      | Show the estimated total and explain that the final amount can change if local taxes or disclosed fees change. |
| Incidentals authorization | A temporary card authorization may be placed at arrival. The amount is property-specific and may be increased if charges are posted.   | It is a hold, not a final charge. Release timing depends on the issuer after checkout.                         |
| Card guarantee            | The card must be valid and authorized under the reservation terms. A failed guarantee requires prompt guest contact or manager review. | Use a valid card or an approved payment method before the cancellation deadline when required.                 |

AuroraStay does not store full payment card numbers in guest notes. Staff must use the approved payment system and follow PCI DSS controls. Never request a card number by email or ordinary chat.

## 6. Reference and review notes

This baseline should be reviewed with Revenue Management, Finance, Guest Experience, and each property leader before publishing a new rate plan. Review state and local requirements for lodging taxes, fee disclosures, cancellation restrictions, accessibility, and consumer protection before changing the rule.

Regulatory alignment to confirm during review includes the Federal Trade Commission's rules and guidance on clear pricing and unfair or deceptive practices, applicable state automatic renewal or fee disclosure requirements, and the Americans with Disabilities Act Title III requirements for places of lodging. The property owner remains responsible for local implementation.

## 7. Cancellation and change workflow

| Step                    | Owner            | Required check                                                                  | Record                                 |
|-------------------------|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 1. Locate reservation   | Any agent        | Match name, confirmation, property, dates, rate plan, and booking channel.      | Reservation ID                         |
| 2. Read rate terms      | Any agent        | Confirm cancellation deadline, charge, payment status, and restrictions.        | Rate rule quoted to guest              |
| 3. Check property rule  | Property team    | Confirm property-specific policy, event restriction, or operational limitation. | Profile or manager name                |
| 4. Complete or escalate | Agent or manager | Issue new confirmation, process eligible refund, or escalate exception.         | Action, amount, approver, guest notice |

Escalate when the confirmation conflicts with the property profile, the booking channel will not release a charge, the guest alleges discrimination or disability-related harm, the guest disputes an unauthorized charge, or the requested exception could create a legal or safety issue.

Document decisions in neutral language. Do not record sensitive medical details, full payment card data, or assumptions about a guest's identity or intent.
